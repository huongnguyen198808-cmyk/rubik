Beginner's CFOP & Notation Guide

Master the fundamental notations and step-by-step layer method to solve any Rubik's Cube.

Standard Notations

R / R'

Right face (Clockwise / Counter-clockwise)

L / L'

Left face (Clockwise / Counter-clockwise)

U / U'

Up / Top face (Clockwise / Counter-clockwise)

D / D'

Down / Bottom face (Clockwise / Counter-clockwise)

F / F'

Front face (Clockwise / Counter-clockwise)

B / B'

Back face (Clockwise / Counter-clockwise)

Layer-by-Layer Steps

1. The White Cross

Form a daisy on the yellow center, then align edge pieces with corresponding side centers and drop them to the bottom to form the white cross.

2. First Layer Corners (F2L Part 1)

Insert white corner pieces into the bottom layer using standard right-hand algorithms (R U R' U') until the first layer is complete.

3. Second Layer Edges

Position middle layer edge pieces using front algorithms to slot them properly between adjacent centers.

4. Top Layer (LL) & Permutation

Form the yellow cross on top, orient yellow corners (OLL), and permute the final corners and edges (PLL) to complete the cube.

Popular Algorithms

Right Trigger (Sexy Move) R U R' U'
Left Trigger L' U' L U
Sune (OLL) R U R' U R U2 R'
T-Perm (PLL) R U R' U' R' F R2 U' R' U' R U R' F'
